About

Hi, I'm Aidan Semple, a Mechatronics Engineering candidate (Class of 2030) at the University of Waterloo. I enjoy building systems that bring together hardware, software, mechanical design, and electrical circuitry to solve real-world problems.

I'm driven by a genuine passion for learning and enjoy picking up new tools, technologies, and design approaches. I develop these skills through coursework, personal projects, and hands-on experimentation. I'm especially interested in pursuing engineering work related to the environment and sustainability, where thoughtful design can make a meaningful impact.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy spending time outdoors, staying active, and playing basketball, which helps me stay balanced and energized. I value collaboration, clear communication, and bringing a positive attitude to the teams I work with.

I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, build, and contribute as I continue developing into a well-rounded engineer.
